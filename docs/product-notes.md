\# Nourish Product Notes



\## Vision



Nourish is an AI-powered nutrition companion that helps people understand their food and make healthier decisions.



\## Problem



Most nutrition apps focus on calorie logging. Users have to manually track everything, and many people do not understand what their food choices mean.



\## Competitors



\### Lose It!

Strengths:

\- Weight loss tracking

\- Calorie counting

\- Food database

\- Barcode scanning



Weakness:

\- Requires manual tracking

\- Less focused on understanding food choices



\### MyFitnessPal

Strengths:

\- Huge food database

\- Established user base



Weakness:

\- Can feel like a tracking spreadsheet



\## Nourish Differentiation



Potential directions:



1\. AI Food Intelligence

\- Take a picture of food

\- Understand nutrition

\- Explain ingredients

\- Give personalized suggestions



2\. AI Nutrition Coach

\- Personalized recommendations

\- Daily insights

\- Habit building



3\. Cultural Food Understanding

\- Better support for homemade and diverse meals

\- Example: Indian foods and mixed dishes



\## Possible Screens



\- Onboarding

\- Home Dashboard

\- Food Scanner

\- AI Analysis

\- Meal History

\- Profile



\## Questions To Decide



\- Who is the main user?

\- Is Nourish focused on weight loss, health, or food understanding?

\- What is the one feature users remember?
